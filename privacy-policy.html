Dock Ferry Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Dock Ferry ("the game") is developed by Wooseok Lee, an individual developer. This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information stored on your device

In the mobile app, your level, coins and harbor decorations are saved only on your device. In the web version on CrazyGames, the same progress is saved through the CrazyGames SDK so it can follow your CrazyGames account. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect your name, email, contacts or location.

Gameplay analytics

The mobile app and the web version use ByteBrew analytics to understand how players progress, so we can tune difficulty and the coin economy. ByteBrew receives a random player ID stored by the game, the level number and result (start, clear, fail), your coin balance and coins spent, items you build, reward-ad results, how many days since you first played, session times, your IP address and country, language, screen size and general device or browser information. It does not receive your name, email or advertising ID, and this data is not used for advertising. See ByteBrew's policy: https://docs.bytebrew.io/BBSettings/privacypolicy. In the web version you can stop this by clearing or blocking cookies for the game; in the mobile app, clearing the app's data or reinstalling it resets the player ID.

Advertising

The mobile app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and general device information to show and measure ads. In regions where consent is required, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

The web version on CrazyGames shows ads provided by CrazyGames under its own privacy policy: https://www.crazygames.com/privacy-policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
